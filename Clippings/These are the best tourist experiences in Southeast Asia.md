A cruise through Vietnam's Ha Long Bay or Lan Ha Bay claimed the top spot on the list.

Typically lasting two to three nights, the cruises accommodate guests in private cabins and offer itineraries that blend relaxation, sightseeing, activities and dining.

Adventurous travelers can go kayaking, swimming or cycling through traditional fishing villages during shore excursions, while others may prefer to unwind on deck and enjoy the spectacular seascapes.

Many cruises also feature cooking demonstrations and cultural performances as part of the onboard experience. Prices start from around US$53 per adult.

Angkor Wat sunrise or sunset tour in Siem Reap ranked second.

The carefully planned experience combines visits to some of the region's most iconic temples with excellent photography opportunities and a flavorful local lunch.

Along the way, guests gain insight into the site's rich history, uncover lesser-known architectural details, and hear fascinating stories and legends that bring Cambodia's ancient heritage to life, Tripadvisor recommended.

Angkor Wat, a temple complex and tourism symbol in Cambodia, spans 162 hectares and is home to ancient temples and monuments. It is considered the largest religious structure in the world by Guinness World Records.

A private tour to explore Ubud, a famous town on Indonesia's resort island Bali, came in third.

Ubud is widely regarded as Bali's cultural and spiritual center, known for its verdant rice terraces, vibrant artistic heritage and thriving wellness scene. The town attracts visitors seeking a blend of natural beauty, traditional Balinese culture and holistic retreat experiences.

A full-day tour to the northern province of Ninh Binh from Hanoi ranked fourth.

According to Tripadvisor, visitors on the tour will visit Hoa Lu, once capital of Vietnam, explore Mua Cave and take in views from the mountain above, ride a boat through the Tam Coc caves, and cycle through the fields to Bich Dong Pagoda cave temples.

The trip includes a restaurant lunch and small-group transfers by limousine minivan.

A 5 traditional dishes cooking class with market trip in Hanoi ranked fifth.

Foreign travelers explore a traditional market, handpick ingredients, and cook iconic Vietnamese dishes like rice vermicelli with grilled pork *bun cha* and spring rolls, gaining cultural insights through a four-hour experience in Hanoi.

Visitors can enjoy a hands-on cooking session as they prepare several classic Vietnamese dishes under expert guidance, which they will enjoy afterward alongside traditional rice wine.

With multiple departure times available, the experience offers flexibility while providing an authentic introduction to Hanoi's rich culinary culture.

Coming at sixth is canyoning in Da Lat, which offers an adrenaline-filled adventure that combines outdoor exploration with spectacular natural scenery. Participants descend cascading waterfalls by abseiling, leap from rocky cliffs into natural pools and trek through lush forest landscapes under the guidance of experienced instructors.

Along the route, guides introduce visitors to a variety of local medicinal and edible plants, adding an educational element to the journey. Aside from a lunch break to recharge, the day is packed with action, making it one of Vietnam's most exciting adventure experiences.

Seventh on the list is Phang Nga Bay Sea Cave Canoeing & James Bond Island by Big Boat in Thailand.

With its turquoise waters, towering limestone karsts, pristine beaches and dense tropical jungle, Phang Nga Bay is one of Thailand's most picturesque coastal destinations. This all-inclusive day tour offers a chance to explore its stunning islands alongside an experienced guide.

The journey includes kayaking through sea caves and mangrove forests at Koh Panak, discovering the dramatic rock formations of Hong Island, and taking in the iconic scenery of James Bond Island. Guests can also unwind on the sandy shores of Naka Island before enjoying a buffet lunch served aboard the boat, making for a memorable day of sightseeing and relaxation.

Finishing the top eight is an evening street food tour through Kuala Lumpur to discover the flavors of Malaysia.

Led by a knowledgeable guide, the experience takes visitors beyond the usual tourist hotspots to explore neighborhoods and culinary gems that many travelers overlook.

One stop is Chow Kit Market, which offers a variety of beloved Malaysian specialties, including flame-grilled chicken satay served with rich peanut sauce. Limited to just eight participants, the small-group tour offers a personalized experience and deep insight into the city's vibrant food culture.

Tripadvisor's Top Experiences ranking is part of its annual Travellers' Choice Best of the Best Awards. Of more than eight million listed experiences, fewer than 1% qualify based on the <font color="#a5a5a5">volume</font> <mark style="background:#d3f8b6">and quality of traveler reviews</mark> collected over the preceding 12 months.
